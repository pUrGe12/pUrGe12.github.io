## The Book

This was during a time where I was so into engineering that I started a spree of reading about all the greatest engineering minds. Steve Woz was a wizard in his craft and in this book he describes how and why he did what he did.

The book kinda reminds me of Wimpy Kid to be honest, where Jobs is kinda like Greg Heffley and Wozniack like Rowley. Because Woz **is** very soft spoken, trusting and smart at electronics (okay maybe Rowley wasn't that smart at all) which is in polar contrast to Jobs.

I don't have a specific thing which I took away from the book except maybe one thing which is that, my man read a lot of documentation. Even he didn't know things at first and there was no easy way back then. Imagine reading all the documentation, laying the memory addresses, writing the firmware, all by hand before anyone can come and help you.

That's massive respect man, I have never tried it because I dread that. I don't even know how that might end up being, reading a documentation for a chip or something! Anyways, love the guy, got some more stories about Apple, both the founders' individual personalities and so on.
